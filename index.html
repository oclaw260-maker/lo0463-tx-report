LO0463 recorded 42 no-stop direction changes in 5.8 h of moving time (7.2/hr). Direct gear-skip transitions (F→R or R→F with no Neutral) = 119. Coach operator: bring machine to a complete stop (Neutral + brake) before reversing direction.
Watch
🔴 Stop using declutch while moving forward / on grade
57 improper declutch events (declutch engaged while moving forward and/or on grade). Comparison loader: 0. Operator should only declutch at standstill (or use inching pedal at very low speed).
Action
🔴 Inspect torque-converter lockup clutch
Mean torque-converter slip with lockup engaged: 10.2% vs comparison 4.8%. Healthy lockup should be ~0–3%. Inspect lockup clutch friction material; check transmission oil for debris.
